# Services Storybook — Source and Rights Log

## Rights principles

- Research references inform interaction, material, and composition decisions; they are not assets for reproduction.
- No third-party artwork, screenshots, decorative assets, or photographs are approved for production use.
- Generated and procedurally rendered variants must be original and must not imitate Viberole or any named artist.
- Final interface copy remains live HTML text.
- Any future commissioned photography requires documented permission, model/property releases where applicable, and a production usage agreement.
- Before launch, confirm the image generator's current commercial-use terms for the account and model used.
